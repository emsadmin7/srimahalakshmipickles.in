About us

Made the way ammamma made it

Sri Mahalakshmi Pickles & Spices is a home-grown Andhra pickle and podi kitchen based in Etcherla, Srikakulam. Every jar is pressed in small batches using cold-pressed oil and sun-cured spices, following recipes passed down through the family — no preservatives, no shortcuts.

  • 🌿 28 recipes across pickles, podi & karam
  • 🧴 Packed fresh to order in 250g–2kg jars
  • 🚚 Delivered across Andhra Pradesh & beyond
