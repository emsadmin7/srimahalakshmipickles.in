Refund & Return Policy

At Sri Mahalakshmi Pickles, as we sell perishable food items, returns are strictly limited for hygiene and safety.
  • Non-returnable: Opened or delivered food products cannot be returned.
  • Damaged / wrong item: Eligible for a full refund or free replacement.
  • Reporting time: Must notify us within 24 hours of delivery.
  • Proof required: Unboxing video or photo showing the issue and shipping label.
  • Refund time: Approved refunds are processed to your original payment method in 5 to 7 days.
  • Cancellation: Orders can only be cancelled before dispatch.
